Our Story

Passed down a needle at a time

I learned to sew at my grandmother's side, and that's still where every Sam & Maddie piece starts — one seam, pressed and pieced by hand, no shortcuts. Now I'm teaching my own daughters the same way, on the same machine, in the same small studio.

"Creating has always been where I feel most at home."

— Samantha, maker & owner

How It's Made

From bolt to bag, by hand

Cut & Piece

100% cotton fabrics are washed, cut, and pieced to pattern before a single seam is sewn.

Quilt & Sew

Batting is layered and quilted, then every bag is assembled on the machine seam by seam.

Finish by Hand

Zippers, magnets, and linings are set and pressed before each piece gets a final check.
